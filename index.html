THE ATELIER'S STORY

A SANCTUARY OF EPICUREAN ART
Founded in the heart of maritime luxury, Nereo is more than a resturant — it is an atelier where pristine sea delicacies are handled like precious sculpture. We collaborate intimately with certified sustainable small-boat dayboat fishermen, importing the finest catch at dawn, cooked within hours.
Every composition reflects deep culinary heritage, harmonized with avant-grade modern techniques under candlelight, providing an elite escape into fine food curation.
